Lithology MapUnitPolys feature class represents the distribution of geologic units. Additional fields have been added to the NCGMP MapUnitPolys to support map publication and query services via ArcGIS Server.The MapUnitAge field references SymbolAge domain. This domain offers a standardized vocabulary for symbolizing stratigraphic age, volcanic and plutonic rocks across the map area. The age domain values correspond to the divisions of geologic time from USGS Fact Sheet 2007-3015, Divisions of Geologic Time – Major Chronostratigraphic and Geochronological Units. Volcanic and plutonic domain values correspond to the terms captured in Appendix A. Terms and Definitions for the NGMDB Geologic Materials Classification of A Classification of Geologic Materials for Web Display of National and Regional-Scale Mapping, USGS Open-File Report 2009-1298.The MapUnitLith field references SymbolLith domain. This domain offers a standardized vocabulary for symbolizing general lithology, as captured in Appendix A. Terms and Definitions for the NGMDB Geologic Materials Classification of USGS Open-File Report 2009-1298, A Classification of Geologic Materials for Web Display of National and Regional-Scale Mapping.Symbology for unit age and general lithology is included in the template as layer and style files. Colors for stratigraphic age are the RGB equivalent of the CMYK values defined in Section 33 of the FGDC Digital Cartographic Standard for Geologic Map Symbolization. Patterns for general lithology have been informed by the standard, in an attempt to mimic the hierarchal system established by Appendix A.
